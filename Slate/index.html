PREP. LIFT. DEFINE.

Your style starts here. Build your routine with
texture, volume, and a matte finish.

01

ADD TEXTURE

Spray Sea Salt Spray onto damp hair. Work through with your fingers, then air-dry for natural movement or blow-dry for added lift.

02

Build Volume

Apply a little Volume Powder at the roots of dry hair. Work in with your fingertips and shape for extra lift.

03

Make it yours

Warm a small amount of Matte Clay between your palms. Work through dry hair, then shape and define your finish.
