What is an eSIM?

An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital version of a physical SIM card — built directly into your device. Instead of inserting or replacing SIM cards when you travel, you can simply download an eSIM profile online and get connected instantly

.With an eSIM, you can activate a local or global data plan in minutes — no shipping, no waiting, and no need to visit a store. It’s the smarter, more sustainable way to stay connected while you travel.

  • No physical SIM required
  • Instant setup via QR code
  • Works in 150+ destinations
  • Eco-friendly and secure

We understand that plans can change unexpectedly. That’s why you can request a refund under certain conditions:

– If you purchased the eSIM without checking if your device is compatible.

– If you canceled your trip or no longer need the eSIM.

Important: Refunds are only possible if the eSIM has not been activated and has remained inactive for less than 180 days since purchase. If the eSIM has already been used or expired, a refund cannot be issued.

Once approved, refunds will be sent to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
